# Story notes: Skerra Rock

Facts every scene must keep.

- **Narrator:** Ines Calder, keeper of the lighthouse on Skerra Rock. First person, past tense.
- **Dog:** Bram, an old grey lurcher, deaf in his left ear. He sleeps by the stove.
- **The lamp:** dark for three nights. The replacement part arrives on the supply boat, which is still four days away.
- **Season and weather:** late October, a gale from the northwest, rain coming sideways.
- **The causeway:** walkable only for two hours either side of low tide. Tonight's low tide was at 21:40, so it closed at 23:40.
- **Isolation:** Ines has not spoken to another person in eleven days. The radio has been dead since the storm began.
- **End of scene 1:** just after midnight, Ines sees a hand-held light moving along the causeway toward the rock, after the tide has closed it.

## Tone and open questions

- **Tone:** quiet, restrained, uncanny. Scene 1 turned supernatural with one wrong detail: a light crossing a causeway that is underwater. Strangeness comes through small, concrete details that don't add up, never through explanation.
- **Ines's voice:** practical and understated. She notices more than she says, and she doesn't dramatise.
- **Keep open:** who the visitor is, how they crossed, why their light is so steady. Don't explain, rationalise or resolve any of these.
